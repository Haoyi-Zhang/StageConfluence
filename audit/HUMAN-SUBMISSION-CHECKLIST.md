# Human submission checklist

- Read every theorem, proof, figure, table, and citation in the final PDF.
- Verify current venue scope, page/format rules, anonymity policy, artifact process, and supplementary-material limits in the live submission portal.
- Confirm author names, affiliations, conflicts, ORCIDs, funding, acknowledgments, copyright, and data/code licenses.
- Make the required disclosure for generative-AI assistance under the policy in force at submission time.
- Upload the exact hashed files listed in `SHA256SUMS.txt`; do not substitute an older PDF or artifact.
- Do not describe the work as accepted, independently peer reviewed, fully mechanized, industrially validated, or performance-improving unless new evidence supports that statement.
